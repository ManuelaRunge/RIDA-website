# Project guidance

## Purpose

This is the static website for RIDA, Manuela Runge's professional technical platform and collaboration doorway. It is not positioned as a full-service agency or as a competing global-health consultancy.

## Positioning boundaries

- Keep the focus on epidemiological research, infectious-disease modelling, computational analysis, technical quality, evidence synthesis and scientific writing.
- Describe RIDA as a technical platform and point of contact for research ideas, technical questions and potential collaboration.
- Preserve the wording that enquiries may be referred to or developed with relevant institutional partners.
- Do not name or imply endorsement by an employer or institutional partner unless explicitly requested and approved.
- Do not add claims about accepting all commercial engagements, maintaining a team, or providing comprehensive programme implementation or organisational strategy.
- Keep AI positioning balanced: tools may support workflows, while domain judgement, methodological scrutiny, verification and accountability remain human-led.

## Technical constraints

- Keep the project static and dependency-free unless a new capability genuinely requires otherwise.
- Preserve responsive behaviour and accessibility.
- Keep body text readable and links keyboard accessible.
- Do not add analytics, trackers, cookies, forms or external integrations without explicit instruction.
- Do not add a framework merely to edit content or styling.

## Validation

After changes:

- Open the page locally and inspect desktop and mobile layouts.
- Confirm that navigation, external links and the email link work.
- Run `node --check script.js` when JavaScript changes.
- Check that the page has no accidental horizontal scrolling.
